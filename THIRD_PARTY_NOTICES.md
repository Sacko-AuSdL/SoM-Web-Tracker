## Earlier tracker and dependencies

This browser tracker uses/adapts data, logic, and structure derived from or
informed by **SoM-Open_Mode-Tracker**, by Cyb3R and contributors. Its MIT
copyright and permission notice is preserved in
[public/POPTRACKER_LICENSE.txt](public/POPTRACKER_LICENSE.txt).
Source snapshots live in `source/poptracker`; generated browser data and
accepted map/item images are identified in `source/provenance.json`.
The tracker package's notice does not license underlying game artwork.

Secret of Mana Randomizer 1.49, by Moppleton and contributors, informed settings,
event, and travel mappings. Its local repository `LICENSE` contains GNU LGPL
version 2.1. This project's MIT notice does not relicense Randomizer material.
No Randomizer executable/library is bundled or linked; its source is not a
runtime dependency and was not modified for this project.
The local Open World Sandship extension also informed optional location/event
identities and minimal compatibility signatures; the inspected files and
flag/prize model are recorded in `verification/sandship-source-and-verification.md`
and `source/provenance.json`. No wholesale Randomizer source was copied.
Dependency packages retain their respective licenses supplied with those
packages. `package-lock.json` records their versions.
